## Additional policy evidence and release action

Fesliyan’s current [policy](https://www.fesliyanstudios.com/faq) distinguishes noncommercial and commercial use, says commercial use requires a donation/license and supplies license documentation afterward. Its game-use FAQ does not establish that this project obtained the required permission or that the bundled edited/different-length WAV matches the credited track. Locate the exact original and any project license/receipt, or obtain suitable rights before distribution. Attribution lead: Background music via https://www.FesliyanStudios.com.

For each other candidate source, download the archive/audio in an authorized review environment, match hashes or decoded PCM with documented legitimate conversions, retain the source-page license offer and contributor chain, record an applicable license choice where alternatives exist, and satisfy attribution/notice/source/share-alike requirements as applicable. If a recording cannot be identified, obtain permission or replace/remove it in a separately authorized asset pass. Do not clear a file because its filename/length resembles a download or because a source page offers CC0.

The YouTube coin source does not establish original recording/game-sound rights. walk.wav, hit.wav and step.wav have no verified credit. Reserved voices, step.wav and start.wav remain distribution blockers even though no native gameplay uses them. Existing artwork and Lidia sprite-license-selection blockers remain unchanged.
